The traditional way

Why we fry in beef tallow

We fry in traditional beef tallow because it performs beautifully at high temperatures and gives our fish and chips their crisp, golden finish and rich, savoury flavour.

Tallow’s fatty-acid composition also makes it naturally resistant to oxidation during frying.

It’s an old-school ingredient chosen for flavour, frying performance and simplicity.
